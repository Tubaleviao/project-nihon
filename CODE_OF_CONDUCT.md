# Code of Conduct

Project Nihon is built in the open by people who disagree about design and still have to ship
together. This code of conduct applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any
community channel run by the project.

## Our standard

- Be respectful. Argue with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give feedback that can be acted on, and take it the same way.
- Welcome newcomers and people for whom English is not the first language. The game ships in
  English and Brazilian Portuguese, and issues and discussion may be written in either.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, slurs, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery,
doxxing or publishing someone's private information, sustained disruption of a discussion, and
encouraging any of these.

## Scope of responsibility

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and
may restrict or block a person who does so repeatedly or seriously. Decisions are explained to the
person concerned unless doing so would put someone at risk.

## Reporting

Report a problem by emailing the maintainers at the address in the repository owner's GitHub
profile, or by using GitHub's "Report content" on the comment itself. Reports are handled
privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.
